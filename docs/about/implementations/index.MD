# Unified Biomedical Knowledge Graph (UBKG)

# Implementations of the UBKG

---

The [UBKG system](/about/system) has been used to build a number of implementations of the
UBKG. 

An implementation includes [distributions](/about/glossary/#ubkg-distribution) of the UBKG with a 
particular [context](/about/contexts).

Distributions are available from the [UBKG Download site](https://ubkg-downloads.xconsortia.org/).

# Petagraph
---
**Petagraph** was a collaboration between the [University of Pittsburgh Department of Biomedical Informatics](https://www.dbmi.pitt.edu/) and the 
[Children's Hospital of Philadelphia's Department of Biomedical and Health Informatics](https://www.research.chop.edu/).

# HuBMAP/SenNet ontology
---
The HuBMAP/SenNet ontology graph (HMSN) supports applications of 
the [HuBMAP](https://hubmapconsortium.org/) and [SenNet](https://sennetconsortium.org/) consortia.

[UBKG-CSV](/ubkg-csv)
